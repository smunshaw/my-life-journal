Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

MyLife Journal is published by Stephen Sinclair Entertainment. Protecting the privacy of the memories, reflections, photographs, goals, events, and other personal information you choose to record is an important part of the design of the application.

Information We Collect

Stephen Sinclair Entertainment does not collect personal data from MyLife Journal.

The journal information you create within the application is intended to remain under your control.

This may include information such as:

  • Journal entries and memories
  • Life events and milestones
  • Goals and reflections
  • Tags and categories
  • Favorites
  • Photographs
  • Drawings
  • Dates associated with your memories or events

Stephen Sinclair Entertainment does not receive or maintain this journal content on its servers.

Personal Journal Content

Your journal content is personal. Stephen Sinclair Entertainment does not access, sell, rent, share, or use the content of your journal for advertising or marketing purposes.

Photos and Media

MyLife Journal may allow you to select photographs or other media from your device for inclusion with your journal memories or life events.

Access to your photo library occurs only when you choose to use features requiring that access and is subject to the permissions you grant through iOS.

Device Permissions

Certain MyLife Journal features may require access to capabilities provided by your device. When required, iOS will request your permission.

You may manage these permissions at any time through your device's Settings application.

Data Sharing

Stephen Sinclair Entertainment does not sell your personal information or journal content.

We do not share your journal content with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies.

Advertising

MyLife Journal does not use your journal content for targeted advertising.

Analytics and Tracking

MyLife Journal does not use your journal content to track you across applications or websites owned by other companies.

Data Storage and Backups

Journal information may be stored locally on your device. If MyLife Journal provides backup or export functionality, any resulting files are controlled by you and may be stored in locations you select.

If you choose to store or transfer a backup using a third-party service, including services provided by Apple, that service's privacy practices and terms may apply to the information stored there.

Data Security

MyLife Journal is designed to keep your personal journal information under your control. However, no electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, device passcode, Apple Account, backups, and any exported copies of your journal.

Children's Privacy

MyLife Journal is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children on behalf of Stephen Sinclair Entertainment.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as MyLife Journal develops and new features are introduced.

When material changes are made, the effective date shown on this page will be updated.

About MyLife Journal

MyLife Journal is a personal journaling application designed to help you capture the moments, memories, goals, reflections, and significant events that together tell the story of your life.

The application provides tools for recording everyday memories, identifying meaningful life events and milestones, organizing experiences with tags, preserving photographs and drawings, and exploring your personal history over time.
